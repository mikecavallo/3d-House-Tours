About the home

A move-in ready Cape in Middlebury.

Listed at $439,900 · reduced from $455,900 on Oct 2

This nicely updated New England Cape has a comfortable, easy layout. The living room is built around a fireplace, and the dining area flows into a large kitchen with tile floors, plenty of cabinets and workspace, and a door straight out to the big back deck.

Two bedrooms and a recently renovated full bath finish the main level, all on hardwood floors. Upstairs is a remodeled full bath, a third bedroom, and a large primary bedroom with a vaulted ceiling, a big closet, and a slider to a balcony over the landscaped backyard.

The full basement walks out into a one-car garage, with lots of storage. There's a large shed and parking for six. It's close to Route 84 and the Middlebury walking trail, in the award-winning Region 15 school district.
