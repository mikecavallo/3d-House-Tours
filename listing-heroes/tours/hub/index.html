Listing pages from 360 tours

Five houses, five ways in.

Same raw material for every house: the panoramas from its 360 tour, color corrected, and the tour's own door links. Each page turns that into a different idea, drawn from the house itself.

1146 Florida Street · San Francisco

The Mission House

The house starts as a planet and unrolls into a walk.

Scroll and the rooftop planet flattens into the terrace view, then walks down through all three levels on the tour's real doors. A live floor plan shows where you stand and which way you face.

4250 23rd Street · Noe Valley

The Noe Valley Victorian

An 1893 house shown as an 1890s photo album.

Every room is a cabinet card whose print develops from sepia into color as you reach it. A brass slider runs from 1893 to today. Open a card and you are standing in the room.

  • Period object as interface
  • Print development

Vine Leaf Residence

An empty new build that starts as a drawing.

The rooms appear as an architect's ink drawing, and your cursor carries the daylight that turns them into photos. Rooms are numbered like a drawing set, and a slider shows the photo correction before and after.
