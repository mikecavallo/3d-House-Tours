Hidden behind an eye-catching Victorian facade lies 1146 Florida, a three-bedroom, three-and-a-half bath home with nearly 3,000 square feet of living space in the Mission District. Tom McElroy Architecture designed the renovation.

Three levels, an open plan from the bay window to the kitchen, and a landscaped garden with a built-in barbecue, fountain, pergola and fire pit.

A block from the 24th Street corridor: eateries, coffee shops, and the rest of what makes the Mission the Mission.
