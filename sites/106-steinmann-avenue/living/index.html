106 Steinmann Avenue · Middlebury, CT

A move-in ready Cape in Middlebury.

Listed at $439,900, reduced from $455,900 on Oct 2. Taxes $7,968 a year, no HOA.

A nicely updated New England Cape with an easy, comfortable layout. The living room is built around a brick fireplace with built-in shelves, and opens through the dining area to a large kitchen with tile floors and plenty of cabinets.

Two bedrooms and a renovated full bath finish the main level, all on hardwood. Upstairs are a remodeled full bath, a third bedroom, and a primary bedroom with a vaulted ceiling, a large closet and a slider to a balcony over the backyard.

The full basement walks out into a one-car garage. There's a large shed, parking for six, and a big deck off the kitchen. Close to Route 84 and the Middlebury walking trail, in the Region 15 school district.
