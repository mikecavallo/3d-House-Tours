A house of grand proportions

Built in 1893 by architect Wm. Mooser, this home is one of a kind. The main level includes grand proportions, from soaring ceilings to oversized rooms.

The gracious formal dining room, the expansive living room with gas fireplace and the chef’s kitchen have been custom designed. Below, a grand staircase leads to four bedrooms, two baths, a family room and a laundry room. Each bedroom has its own view of the lush, verdant garden.
