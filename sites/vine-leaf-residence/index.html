G-001General notes

An empty house, drawn and lit room by room.

8902 Vine Leaf sits on the edge of open land in Schertz, Texas. Every room was photographed in 360. Here is what the photographs show.

  1. Tile floors run through the foyer, halls, kitchen, dining and living spaces.
  2. White shaker cabinets with granite counters, a large island and pendant lights in the kitchen.
  3. Arched openings, plantation shutters and a fireplace in the living room.
  4. Carpeted bedrooms, a primary suite with a bay of windows, and a walk-in closet with built-in shelving.
  5. A walk-in shower with a herringbone tile band and a double vanity.
  6. A covered back porch that looks over open land and a vineyard row.

Notes describe what is visible in the photographs only. Ask for specifications, plans and measurements at the showing.
